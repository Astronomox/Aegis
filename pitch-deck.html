How AEGIS Fires

The phone stays locked. Two sensors detect the attack. Your family gets alerted in seconds.

01

Accelerometer + Gyroscope

Captures violent motion: snatch, drop, bag insertion. On-device ML classifies the pattern in real time.

02

Audio Classification (Edge TFLite)

Mic detects distress keywords and aggressive tone. Model runs on the phone's neural processing unit. No cloud round-trip.

03

Watcher Dashboard (WebSocket Push)

Family's browser receives alert in under 3 seconds. GPS coordinates, 5-second audio clip, device status.

04

Zero Screen Activity

Screen stays black. No notification sound. No vibration. Attackers see a dead phone.

Built Around the Customer

Trust Is the Product

Nigerians will not install a safety app that records them 24/7. We designed AEGIS so nothing leaves the device unless someone's life is at risk.

01

On-Device Processing

Audio classification runs on the phone's NPU using TensorFlow Lite. No cloud upload. No server sees your data.

02

Auto-Wipe After Processing

Audio buffer clears within 2 seconds of classification. Nothing persists beyond the detection window.

03

Violence-Only Transmission

The 5-second clip sends only when both triggers confirm an attack. False positives stay on-device.

04

Dark by Design

No notification. No LED. No vibration. Attackers see a phone that does nothing. The customer stays safe.

The customer controls the data. Nothing is saved or sent unless their life depends on it.
